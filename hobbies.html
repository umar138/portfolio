PUBG Mobile Online game

PUBG Mobile is a multiplayer battle royale game. Players land on a map, collect equipment, work with teammates and try to be the last player or squad remaining. I enjoy its teamwork, strategy and competitive gameplay.

Football Physical game

Football is a team sport in which two teams try to score by moving the ball into the opponent's goal. It encourages fitness, communication, discipline and teamwork. It is a great way to stay active.
